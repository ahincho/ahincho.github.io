The hero opened with a flat 4rem above it on every screen under 900px. Stacked
on the 65px sticky header, that left a phone with 129 pixels — a sixth of the
screen — before the first word, and pushed the terminal off the fold entirely.
The space is fluid now: 36px on a phone, 54px on a tablet, unchanged on a
desktop, where there was room for it all along.

The GitHub and LinkedIn icons in the hero were 20×20 targets, under the 24 WCAG
asks for and well under what a thumb needs. The icons are the same size; the
target around them is 39×39, grown with padding and given back with a negative
margin so nothing on the line moved.
